## Description

Somewhere in a studio vault, a cartoon from 1933 was never finished. Its star is Morty, a skull with a lot to say, and
the reel has fallen to pieces. You're going to throw him through it.

**Pull down, aim, let go.** Lob Morty through a haunted ring: through clean for a perfect, off the rim for a
rattle-in, and hope the wind is kind. The ring slides, swings, carousels and pops into 3D as the reel goes on.

**Eight maps, sixteen bosses.** From Moonshine Cemetery to the projection booth itself, each map plays its own way,
with its own hazard, its own bonus target, a mini-boss halfway and an end boss holding one of Morty's missing pieces.
Win all eight back to end the reel.

**Ways to play.** Story, Arcade (a haunted penny arcade of cabinets, with high-score initials), Practice, Boss Rush,
three mini-games, and the Director's Challenge: a new note from the head of production every week, the same for
everyone.

**A cartoon that misbehaves.** The print jams, the frame slips, the animator's hand wanders in. Nine secrets are
hidden in it. The Codex and the Production Archive fill in the story of the studio.

**Make him yours.** Hundreds of looks in the Skull Vault (skulls, eyes, teeth, paint, hats, auras, rings, trails and
bands), earned by playing or bought with bones. Signature shots, career levels, the Shot Book, and a daily streak.

**Fair to play.** No energy timers and no pay-to-win: nothing you can buy changes how Morty flies. Bones are earned
by playing. Souls (the premium currency) buy looks only, and come free each day too. Ads are only ever your choice,
for one more skull.

Made for touch, mouse, keyboard and gamepad. Plays offline. Reduced motion, flash, contrast and text-size settings
throughout.
