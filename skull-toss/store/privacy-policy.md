# Skull Toss privacy policy (draft)

*A starting draft written from what the game actually does. Have it reviewed for your jurisdiction before you
publish it, and fill in the bracketed parts.*

**Who we are.** Skull Toss is made by [studio name], [address]. Contact: [email].

**What the game keeps on your device.** Your progress, settings and looks are saved in your browser or the app's
storage so you can pick up where you left off. A copy of your progress is also kept once a day so you can recover it.

**What goes to our server, and why.**

- **An anonymous account.** When the game connects to our server (Google Firebase), it signs you in anonymously. We
  get a random id, not your name, email or phone number.
- **Your cloud save**, so your progress can follow you. It holds your game progress and looks, and nothing else.
- **The leaderboard.** If you finish a Story run, its score, the name you put on your headstone, your title and your
  look are posted, along with a record of the run so we can check it's genuine.
- **Souls.** Your Souls balance, what you bought with it, and a record of each change (including store receipts,
  so a purchase is credited once and refunds can be handled). Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never
  see your payment details.
- **Play data, only if you agree.** If you say yes to "Share play data", we receive anonymous facts about play: how
  runs go, the first time you do each thing, bosses, power-ups, continues, looks bought and worn, challenges claimed,
  errors, and your device's screen size, language and game version. Never your name, your save, anything you type,
  or anything to do with ads. It's kept for 30 days, then deleted. Daily totals, with no ids, are kept longer. You
  can turn it off at any time in Settings. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, we treat
  that as a no.
- **Google Analytics, only if you agree** (the same "Share play data" yes). Google Analytics counts how many people
  play each day and how many come back. It uses a cookie (`_ga`) holding a random id, set only after you've said
  yes, and receives the same anonymous play facts. Advertising features are switched off. Google Analytics doesn't
  store IP addresses, and we keep its data for [2 months].

**Ads (iOS and Android).** Ads are shown only when you choose to watch one (for another skull). They're requested as
non-personalised ads from Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form asks you first. We don't
use your data to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

**Who else is involved.** Google Firebase (hosting, sign-in, database, server functions), Google Analytics (with
your consent), Google AdMob (ads, iOS and Android), Apple and Google (purchases), and Valve (Steam, on the desktop).
We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

**Children.** The game is rated for everyone. It collects no personal information, has no chat, and shows only
non-personalised ads. [If you target children under 13 in the US, review COPPA; for the EU, GDPR-K.]

**Your choices and rights.** Turn play data off in Settings. Reset your progress in Settings. To have your
anonymous account's data deleted, or to ask what we hold, write to [email] with the id shown in Settings → Profile
[add this display if you offer requests by id]. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights (access,
correction, deletion, objection). Contact us to use them.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with its date.
