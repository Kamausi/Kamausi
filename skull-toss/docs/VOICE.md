# Morty's voice (v51)

The casting brief for Mortimer "Morty" Bones, for a voice actor or a voice-design tool. In the game, the
**Spoken** voice setting uses the browser's own speech, cast as near to this as a browser allows (an American
English man's voice, pitched a little low and spoken a little quick: `mortyVoice()` in `src/js/02c_audio_music.js`).

## Brief

Original male cartoon character for a fast-paced arcade game. A mischievous, cocky talking skull from a fictional
1930s theatrical cartoon world.

- **Accent:** light Transatlantic / Mid-Atlantic American, in the manner of 1930s–40s American radio and Hollywood
  performers.
- **Voice:** medium-low pitch, a slightly raspy texture, crisp diction, expressive vowels, excellent comic timing.
- **Character:** charismatic, theatrical, sarcastic, playful, competitive and slightly unhinged. He treats every throw
  like a grand sporting event and believes he's the star of the show.
- **Range:** smug and controlled normally; explosive with excitement after a spectacular hit; briefly panicked or
  incredulous when things go wrong. Dramatic pauses, exaggerated emphasis, quick comic timing, the odd manic burst.
- **Avoid:** strong British pronunciation, a pirate voice, a monster voice, a childlike delivery, heavy distortion,
  constant shouting, or imitating any existing character or actor.

**In one line:** a cocky 1930s cartoon showman trapped inside a skull.

## Lines

Every line he can say is in `src/strings/en.json` under `morty.*` (by situation: boss arrivals, fragments, misses,
perfects, idle chatter). A recorded voice would replace the browser speech line by line, by those IDs.
